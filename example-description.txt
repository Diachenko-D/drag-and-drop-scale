Test Data (Program Walkthrough Description)

1. Application Startup
   When opening the program, the main window is displayed, split into two areas: a control panel on the left, and a canvas on the right with a centered scale platform at the bottom.
   A label reading "0 kg" is displayed on the platform.

2. Object Creation
   After selecting a material from the dropdown list and entering the number of cubes (a positive integer), clicking the "Create cubes" button generates the corresponding squares near the top of the canvas.
   Cubes are arranged in a grid layout (up to 5 per row). If the requested quantity exceeds the generation area bounds, creation stops cleanly without throwing errors.
   (Height is capped at 250 pixels for newly generated rows, indirectly limiting single-batch generation to 10 cubes).

3. Object Dragging (Drag & Drop)
   Holding down the left mouse button over any cube raises it above all other elements and follows the cursor.
   Movement is restricted to canvas boundaries: a cube cannot pass beyond window edges. Releasing the mouse button drops the active selection into gravity physics.

4. Fall Physics Behavior
   Upon release, the cube falls vertically to the nearest solid surface:
   - if its horizontal span overlaps with the scale platform, it falls onto the scale;
   - otherwise, it drops to the bottom boundary of the canvas ("floor").
   Cubes do not collide or stack on top of each other: they do not serve as support structures or block the fall of other objects.

5. Mass Calculation
   A cube's mass contributes to the total calculated weight only when meeting two conditions simultaneously:
   - the cube is located entirely within the horizontal boundaries of the platform;
   - its vertical coordinate sits exactly at the top surface level of the scale (with a tolerance of ±2 pixels).
   When these conditions are met, the cube's mass (proportional to material density) is added to the total value displayed centered on the platform in "X.X kg" format.

6. State Reset
   Clicking the "Reset" button clears all active cubes from the canvas, resets internal program state variables, and resets the platform readout back to "0 kg".

7. Window Resize Adaptation
   When resizing the main application window, the scale platform automatically redraws centered at the bottom of the canvas, preserving its fixed width and height.
   The mass text label remains perfectly centered. The positions of previously created cubes remain unaltered.
